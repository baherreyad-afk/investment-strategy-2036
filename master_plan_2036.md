# 2036 Capital Deployment Master Plan

## Opening Objective and Constraints

The objective is to reach €1.5M by 2036 through consistent capital deployment and disciplined risk control. The constraints are limited annual contributions, the need to survive multi-year drawdowns, and the volatility inherent in crypto markets. This plan was written in a calm state and must be followed mechanically during market stress, without improvisation or reaction to headlines.

## Strategic Architecture: Four Layers

### 1) Base Compounder (Equities / ETFs / Quality Stocks)
- **Target allocation range:** 55%–70%
- **Role in the plan:** Provide steady compounding, liquidity, and structural resilience.
- **What failure looks like:** Chronic underperformance due to excessive concentration or abandoning diversification during volatility.

### 2) Cyclical / Opportunistic Equities
- **Target allocation range:** 10%–20%
- **Role in the plan:** Capture valuation dislocations and business-cycle opportunities without dominating total risk.
- **What failure looks like:** Overtrading, poor timing, or allowing cyclical exposure to exceed the base layer in size.

### 3) Convex Crypto (Core vs Alt Distinction)
- **Target allocation range:** 10%–20%
- **Role in the plan:** Maintain convex upside through a core allocation, with a clearly separated, smaller alt exposure.
- **What failure looks like:** Letting alt positions dictate risk, or holding through excessive drawdowns without trimming.

### 4) Tactical Convex Bets
- **Target allocation range:** 0%–10%
- **Role in the plan:** Time-limited, asymmetric bets funded from profits or excess cash, not from core capital.
- **What failure looks like:** Becoming a permanent allocation or compounding losses due to lack of defined exits.

## Year-by-Year Plan

### 2026 (Foundation Year)
- **Expected capital sources:** Primary income contributions; reserve cash for volatility.
- **What to buy:** Broad equities/ETFs and a small core crypto position.
- **What not to do:** No leverage, no concentrated bets, no frequent reallocations.
- **Key discipline rules:** Automate contributions and rebalance only by schedule.

### 2027 (Accumulation & Discipline)
- **Expected capital sources:** Continued income contributions; minor reallocations from savings.
- **What to buy:** Increase base compounder; add selective cyclicals when valuations are reasonable.
- **What not to do:** No chasing short-term rallies; avoid expanding alt exposure.
- **Key discipline rules:** Maintain allocation ranges and document any deviations before acting.

### 2028 (First Harvest)
- **Expected capital sources:** Partial gains from cyclicals or crypto; ongoing contributions.
- **What to buy:** Reallocate harvested gains to base compounder and cash buffers.
- **What not to do:** No reinvestment of all gains into the highest-volatility assets.
- **Key discipline rules:** Trim into strength and restore target ranges.

### 2029–2030 (Volatility / Drawdown Window)
- **Expected capital sources:** Contributions and optional deployment of cash reserves.
- **What to buy:** Base compounder and selective cyclicals after drawdowns.
- **What not to do:** No averaging down into deteriorating assets; no panic selling.
- **Key discipline rules:** Add risk only after drawdowns and only within defined caps.

### 2031–2033 (Compound & Concentrate)
- **Expected capital sources:** Compounded portfolio gains; steady contributions.
- **What to buy:** Strengthen core positions and concentrate on highest-conviction compounders.
- **What not to do:** No dilution into low-quality opportunities; avoid new speculative themes.
- **Key discipline rules:** Concentration increases only after fundamental validation and trimming elsewhere.

### 2034–2036 (Conversion Phase)
- **Expected capital sources:** Harvested gains from cyclicals and crypto; ongoing compounding.
- **What to buy:** Defensive, liquid assets to secure the target; maintain a reduced convex sleeve.
- **What not to do:** No large new risk at late stage; no abandoning diversification.
- **Key discipline rules:** Prioritize preservation, liquidity, and staged harvesting.

## Aggression vs Conservatism Clarification

This strategy may feel conservative at times because it prioritizes survival through drawdowns and consistency of contributions. The aggression is expressed through controlled convexity, capital recycling, and systematic harvesting rather than constant risk expansion. Asymmetric upside is pursued without risking ruin or compromising the base compounder.

## Risk Management Rules

- **Crypto allocation caps:** Total crypto exposure must remain within the defined 10%–20% range.
- **Convex altcoin caps:** Alt exposure must be a subset of crypto and remain below 5% of total portfolio value.
- **Trimming and harvesting principles:** Trim into strength, redeploy into base compounder or cash, and avoid round-tripping gains.
- **Rules for adding risk only after drawdowns:** Add risk only after a meaningful drawdown, and only if total allocation remains within targets.

## Closing

The philosophy is disciplined compounding with bounded convexity, executed through consistent contributions, scheduled rebalancing, and explicit harvesting rules. This document is the reference point for decisions, not short-term P&L or market sentiment.
